Get the app

Put Campus Clash on your home screen

No app store, no download. Your browser can add Campus Clash to your home screen so it opens like any other app. It takes about 30 seconds.

  • Game alerts. Kickoffs, close finishes and final scores for your teams. On iPhone, alerts only work once the app is on your home screen.
  • Full screen. No address bar or browser tabs.
  • One tap. Opens straight to your standings.

You're already using the installed app. You're all set.

The icon is locked in when you add the app. To change it later, delete Campus Clash from your home screen, pick a new icon here, and add it again.

2Add it to your device

iPhone & iPad

Use Safari. In Chrome on iPhone, Share is at the top right instead.

  1. Open this page in Safari. Install from here, not from the sign-in screen.

  2. Tap the Share button . At the bottom of the screen. If you don't see it, tap ••• first, then Share.

  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen . Not in the list? Tap Edit Actions at the bottom and add it.

  4. Leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add.

  5. Open Campus Clash from the new icon on your home screen and sign in. The app asks you to sign in once, even if Safari is already signed in. That's normal.

Want game alerts? Open the app from the icon, go to My Team, tap the pencil to edit your profile, and turn on Game-day alerts.

Android

Use Chrome.

Or do it by hand:

  1. Open this page in Chrome. Install from here, not from the sign-in screen.

  2. Tap the menu in the top-right corner.

  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install. Some phones call it Install app.

  4. Open Campus Clash from the new icon on your home screen.

Mac or PC

Use Chrome, Edge or Safari.

Or do it by hand:

  1. Chrome or Edge: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.

  2. Safari on a Mac: choose File, then Add to Dock.

  3. Campus Clash opens in its own window and shows up with your other apps.
