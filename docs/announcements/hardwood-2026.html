How it works

Same game you know. A sport built for it.

10

Draft teams, not players

A snake draft, ten rounds, from a pool of the top 120 teams by preseason rating. No injuries, no lineups to set.

Q1

Wins are priced

A team plays about 31 games, so not every win is equal. Beating Duke on the road pays far more than hosting a team ranked 300th.

126

March decides it

The NCAA Tournament pays on every round a team reaches. A national champion banks 126 points in March alone.

The regular season

Every win goes in a quadrant

The same system the selection committee uses to judge a résumé. What a win is worth depends on how good the opponent is and where the game was played.
